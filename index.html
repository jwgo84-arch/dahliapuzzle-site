How it plays

Every puzzle is a blooming dahlia. Each petal holds a word: solve one set of clues reading clockwise and another reading counterclockwise. The answers cross and share letters, so every word you solve helps you crack the next.

Features

  • Easy, everyday clues: no obscure crossword words
  • Puzzles that grow from small blooms to giant dahlias
  • Grow your own garden: every solved puzzle plants new flowers and critters
  • Hints whenever you get stuck
