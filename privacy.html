Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how JoshPan Studios LLC ("we," "us") handles information in the Dahlia Puzzle mobile game ("the App").

Information we collect

We do not require an account, and we do not ask for your name, email address, or other personal information to play.

  • Game progress (levels completed, garden items, hint counts, settings) is stored on your device.
  • Purchases are processed entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see or store your payment details. We receive only confirmation that a purchase was made so we can unlock it.

Advertising

The App does not show ads and does not use advertising identifiers or track you across other apps or websites.

How we use information

  • To save your progress and unlock purchases
  • To fix crashes and improve the game

We do not sell or share your personal information.

Children

The App is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Deleting your data

Because your progress is stored on your device, uninstalling the App deletes it. For any other data questions or deletion requests, email us at the address below.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
