How do I restore my purchases?

Open the game's settings and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in to the same Google Play or Apple account you used to buy.

I lost my progress

Progress is saved on your device, so uninstalling the game or switching phones resets it. Purchases can always be restored.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by the store you bought from: Google Play or the Apple App Store.
